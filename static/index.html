📚 Integrated Speech Corpora

🗺️ Dialect Zones of Kashmir Valley

  • Kamraz (Northern): Spoken in Kupwara & Baramulla districts. Characterized by distinctive vowel height and retroflex articulation.
  • Wular Zone: Spoken around Bandipora and northern lakes with distinctive intonation contours.
  • Maraz (Southern): Spoken in Shopian, Islamabad (Anantnag), and Pulwama with prolonged central vowels (*ٲ*).
